
# Cedar Ridge Services — Management Decisions & Resource Planning

**Assessment:** NIST CSF 2.0 Gap Assessment  
**Planning Period:** November 16, 2026 – February 13, 2027  
**Prepared For:** IT Manager and Executive Management  
**Status:** Proposed for Management Review

## 1. Management Overview

The cybersecurity gap assessment identified seven findings requiring corrective action and four additional areas requiring supporting evidence.

Three findings have been prioritized as High:

- GF-002 — Vulnerability Remediation Tracking
- GF-001 — Inconsistent MFA Enforcement
- GF-003 — Backup and Recovery Validation

These issues present potential risks to system availability, company information, and client services.

The proposed 90-day roadmap addresses urgent exposures first while allowing time to improve documentation, testing, and ongoing security processes.

## 2. 90-Day Remediation Strategy

The remediation roadmap is divided into three phases to address immediate security risks while balancing the company's limited IT resources.

### Phase 1 — Immediate Risk Reduction

**November 16 – December 15, 2026**

The first phase focuses on addressing vulnerabilities and control weaknesses that require immediate attention.

**Key Activities:**

- Investigate overdue critical vulnerabilities and verify remediation.
- Review MFA coverage and undocumented exceptions.
- Investigate failed backup restoration tests.
- Correct incomplete hardware inventory records.
- Address overdue security awareness training.
- Request outstanding evidence for the four Not Assessed outcomes.

**Expected Outcome:** Immediate risks have documented corrective actions, assigned owners, and remediation progress. Outstanding assessment evidence is requested and tracked.

### Phase 2 — Control Improvements

**December 16, 2026 – January 14, 2027**

The second phase focuses on strengthening security procedures and addressing weaknesses identified during the assessment.

**Key Activities:**

- Implement approved MFA requirements and exception management.
- Establish documented recovery objectives and restoration approvals.
- Update incident notification and escalation procedures.
- Conduct an incident response tabletop exercise.
- Formalize security responsibilities and policy review requirements.

**Expected Outcome:** Security processes are more clearly defined, consistently managed, and supported by documented procedures.

### Phase 3 — Validation and Management Review

**January 15 – February 13, 2027**

The final phase focuses on evaluating whether corrective actions have addressed the original findings and communicating the results to management.

**Key Activities:**

- Review remediation evidence and retest applicable controls.
- Update the Current vs. Target Assessment.
- Identify unresolved findings and remaining risks.
- Present remediation progress and outstanding decisions to management.

**Expected Outcome:** Management receives an updated assessment showing completed improvements, remaining risks, and recommended next actions.

## 4. Resource Considerations

Cedar Ridge Services has a small IT team responsible for daily support, system administration, and cybersecurity activities.

The roadmap relies primarily on existing personnel rather than assuming a dedicated remediation team.

### IT Administrator

Responsible for MFA configuration, device management, backup testing, and several evidence requests.

Multiple Phase 1 activities depend on this role, creating a potential workload conflict.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize urgent authentication and recovery work, coordinate supporting activities with the IT Manager, and confirm available capacity before approving deadlines.

### Engineering Lead

Responsible for investigating overdue vulnerabilities affecting cloud-hosted applications.

**Recommendation:** Reserve engineering capacity for urgent vulnerability investigation and remediation verification.

### IT Manager

Responsible for security governance, incident response improvements, recovery objectives, and management reporting.

**Recommendation:** Coordinate remediation activities, monitor deadlines, and escalate unresolved risks.

### Operations Manager

Responsible for employee training follow-up.

**Recommendation:** Coordinate with department managers to resolve overdue and missing training assignments.

Progress reporting should reference the task IDs in `remediation_roadmap.csv`.

## 8. Remediation Validation

A task should not be considered complete solely because the assigned owner reports that corrective action was taken.

Closure should require:

1. Documentation of the corrective action.
2. Supporting evidence showing the change was implemented.
3. Review or testing appropriate to the finding.
4. Confirmation that the original weakness has been addressed.
5. Recorded approval of closure by the designated reviewer.

If testing is unsuccessful, the task should remain open until the issue is resolved or management formally approves an alternative risk treatment.

## 9. Recommended Management Action

Management should review the proposed priorities, confirm available resources, and approve the remediation approach.

Immediate attention is recommended for the overdue critical vulnerability, MFA exceptions, and failed restoration testing.

The IT Manager should maintain visibility into outstanding evidence requests and provide regular progress updates.

The roadmap remains a proposal until the responsible decision-makers confirm priorities, resource commitments, and remediation deadlines.
