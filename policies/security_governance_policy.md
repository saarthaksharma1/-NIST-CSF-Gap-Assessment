
# Cedar Ridge Services
## Information Security Governance Policy

**Policy Owner:** IT Manager  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 20, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** January 20, 2025  
**Status:** Current Policy Under Review

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Cedar Ridge Services' approach to managing cybersecurity responsibilities, security risks, and organizational security policies.

The objective is to protect company and client information while supporting business operations and responsible use of IT resources.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, business systems, and information assets managed by Cedar Ridge Services.

The IT team is responsible for coordinating security activities with department managers and executive management.

## 3. Security Governance

The IT Manager oversees the company's cybersecurity practices and provides security-related updates to executive management when necessary.

Cybersecurity responsibilities are shared across IT, engineering, operations, and department management.

Significant security decisions may require executive management approval.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

**Executive Management**
- Provides oversight of cybersecurity priorities.
- Approves significant security expenditures.
- Makes decisions regarding major business risks.

**IT Manager**
- Oversees security operations and policies.
- Coordinates security assessments.
- Reports significant security concerns to management.
- Recommends corrective actions.

**IT Administrator**
- Manages system access and configurations.
- Supports vulnerability remediation.
- Maintains security-related documentation.

**Engineering Lead**
- Oversees security practices within development environments.
- Coordinates remediation of software-related weaknesses.

**Department Managers**
- Communicate employee access requirements.
- Support implementation of security procedures.

**Employees**
- Follow company security policies.
- Protect company information.
- Report suspected security incidents.

## 5. Cybersecurity Risk Management

Security risks may be identified through internal reviews, vulnerability assessments, incidents, or employee reports.

The IT Manager evaluates identified risks based on their potential likelihood and impact on company operations.

Significant risks are communicated to executive management for review.

Corrective actions are assigned to responsible personnel based on business priorities and available resources.

Risk evaluations and remediation decisions may be documented through internal project tracking systems.

## 6. Security Policies

Cedar Ridge Services maintains policies addressing areas such as:

- Access control and authentication
- Incident response
- Backup and recovery
- Security responsibilities
- Acceptable use of company systems

The IT Manager is responsible for maintaining these policies and communicating relevant changes.

Policies are reviewed when significant operational or technology changes occur.

## 7. Security Awareness

Employees receive cybersecurity guidance during onboarding and through periodic company communications.

Training may address phishing, password security, protection of company information, and reporting suspicious activity.

Department managers assist with communicating security expectations.

## 8. Security Monitoring and Reporting

The IT team monitors relevant security concerns using available system tools and service provider notifications.

Significant vulnerabilities, incidents, and security weaknesses are escalated to the IT Manager.

The IT Manager communicates material security concerns to executive management when appropriate.

## 9. Risk Decisions and Corrective Actions

Management determines whether significant risks require immediate remediation, additional safeguards, or other business decisions.

Corrective actions are coordinated by the IT Manager and assigned to relevant teams.

Progress may be reviewed during operational meetings or other management discussions.

## 10. Policy Maintenance

This policy is maintained by the IT Manager and updated when necessary to reflect changes in business operations, technology, or security requirements.
