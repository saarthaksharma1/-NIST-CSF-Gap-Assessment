
# Cedar Ridge Services
## Access Control and Authentication Policy

**Policy Owner:** IT Manager
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** June 15, 2025
**Last Reviewed:** June 15, 2025
**Status:** Current Policy Under Review

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for managing employee access to Cedar Ridge Services' information systems and business applications.

Its purpose is to reduce unauthorized access to company and client information while supporting normal business operations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, and other authorized users accessing company-managed systems.

In-scope applications include Microsoft 365, GitHub, Jira, Confluence, and cloud-hosted business systems.

## 3. Account Provisioning

New user accounts are created by IT following a request from the employee's manager.

Managers identify the applications and permissions required for each employee's job responsibilities.

IT Administrators provision access based on the request and business needs.

Approval may be documented through email or internal service requests.

## 4. Authentication Requirements

All users must maintain individual accounts and protect their login credentials.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for administrator accounts.

MFA is encouraged for standard employee accounts where supported by the application.

Employees must not share passwords or account credentials.

## 5. Access Permissions

Employees should receive access appropriate to their assigned job responsibilities.

Managers are responsible for notifying IT when employees require additional access or change roles.

Privileged access is restricted to authorized IT and engineering personnel.

IT reviews access permissions periodically for sensitive business applications.

## 6. Employee Termination

Managers are responsible for notifying IT when an employee leaves the organization.

IT removes the employee's application access as promptly as practical after receiving the termination notification.

Account removal activities may be recorded in the organization's internal service management system.

## 7. Access Exceptions

Exceptions to standard access requirements must be reviewed by the IT Manager.

Exceptions may be approved through email when necessary to support business operations.

## 8. Responsibilities

**IT Manager**
- Oversees access management practices.
- Reviews requests for security exceptions.
- Addresses significant access-related concerns.

**IT Administrator**
- Creates, modifies, and disables employee accounts.
- Maintains application access.
- Supports access reviews.

**Department Managers**
- Request employee access.
- Notify IT about role changes and terminations.
- Review permissions when requested.

**Employees**
- Protect account credentials.
- Follow authentication requirements.
- Report suspicious account activity.

## 9. Policy Maintenance

The IT Manager is responsible for updating this policy when significant business or technology changes occur.

Revisions are communicated to affected employees as needed.
