
# Cedar Ridge Services
## Backup and Recovery Policy

**Policy Owner:** IT Manager  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** July 1, 2025  
**Status:** Current Policy Under Review

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for backing up and recovering Cedar Ridge Services' business systems and information.

The objective is to minimize data loss and business disruption resulting from system failures, accidental deletion, cyberattacks, or other operational incidents.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to company-managed systems and business information requiring backup and recovery protection.

In-scope systems include:

- Microsoft 365 business information
- Cloud-hosted applications and databases
- Critical system configurations
- Internal business documentation
- Software development repositories and related records

Third-party cloud services may provide backup and recovery capabilities under their respective service agreements.

## 3. Backup Requirements

The IT team is responsible for identifying systems and information requiring backup protection.

Critical cloud-hosted application databases are scheduled for daily backups.

Important system configurations and business records are backed up regularly based on operational needs.

Backup schedules may vary depending on the system, business requirements, and available cloud service capabilities.

## 4. Backup Storage and Protection

Backup copies are maintained using company-approved storage or cloud services.

Access to backup systems is restricted to authorized IT personnel.

Backup information should be protected against unauthorized access, modification, and deletion.

The IT team is responsible for maintaining appropriate storage capacity and reviewing backup availability.

## 5. Backup Monitoring

IT Administrators monitor backup operations and investigate reported failures.

Backup alerts may be generated through cloud management tools or backup software.

Failed backup jobs should be investigated and corrected as soon as practical.

Significant backup failures are escalated to the IT Manager.

## 6. Backup Retention

Backup information is retained based on business requirements, available storage capacity, and applicable service agreements.

The IT Manager determines whether additional retention requirements are necessary for critical business information.

Outdated backup copies may be removed when they are no longer required.

## 7. Recovery Procedures

When a system failure or security incident affects business operations, the IT Manager coordinates recovery activities.

Recovery activities may include:

1. Identifying the affected systems and information.
2. Determining whether restoration is required.
3. Selecting an available backup.
4. Restoring affected data or system configurations.
5. Verifying basic system functionality.
6. Returning the affected service to normal operations.

Recovery priorities are determined based on operational impact and the availability of IT resources.

## 8. Restoration Testing

The IT team performs restoration testing periodically to confirm that backup information can be recovered.

Testing may include restoring sample files, application data, or system configurations.

Test results should be documented when practical.

Any identified restoration failures are escalated to the IT Manager for corrective action.

## 9. Responsibilities

**IT Manager**
- Oversees backup and recovery practices.
- Determines recovery priorities.
- Reviews significant backup and restoration issues.
- Coordinates recovery during major disruptions.

**IT Administrator**
- Configures and monitors backup operations.
- Investigates backup failures.
- Performs restoration activities and testing.
- Maintains backup-related documentation.

**Operations Manager**
- Communicates critical business recovery requirements.
- Coordinates operational activities during prolonged disruptions.

**Employees**
- Store business information using approved systems.
- Report suspected data loss or system availability issues.

## 10. Policy Maintenance

The IT Manager reviews this policy when significant changes occur to company systems, business operations, or backup technology.

Updates are communicated to relevant personnel.
