
# Cedar Ridge Services
## Incident Response Policy

**Policy Owner:** IT Manager  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** August 1, 2025  
**Status:** Current Policy Under Review

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the process for identifying, reporting, investigating, and responding to cybersecurity incidents affecting Cedar Ridge Services.

The objective is to minimize business disruption, protect company and client information, and restore normal operations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, company-managed devices, cloud applications, and business systems.

Incidents may include:

- Unauthorized access to company accounts
- Phishing and credential compromise
- Malware or ransomware infections
- Loss or disclosure of sensitive information
- Disruption of critical business applications

## 3. Incident Reporting

Employees are responsible for reporting suspected cybersecurity incidents to the IT team through email or the internal help desk.

Reports should include relevant details, such as affected systems, unusual activity, and the approximate time of discovery.

The IT Manager determines whether further investigation is required.

## 4. Incident Assessment

The IT team investigates reported incidents and evaluates their potential impact on company operations.

Incidents are generally categorized as low, medium, or high severity based on the affected systems and business impact.

The IT Manager determines the appropriate response based on the available information.

## 5. Incident Containment

IT personnel may take actions to prevent additional harm, including:

- Disabling compromised accounts
- Resetting employee credentials
- Isolating affected devices
- Restricting access to compromised applications
- Working with cloud service providers

Containment activities should be coordinated with the IT Manager when practical.

## 6. Incident Communication

The IT Manager communicates significant security incidents to executive management.

Management determines whether customers, vendors, or other external parties need to be notified.

External communications must be approved by executive management.

## 7. Recovery

After an incident is contained, the IT team works to restore affected systems and services.

Recovery activities may include restoring backups, rebuilding devices, and verifying application functionality.

Business operations resume when the IT Manager determines that affected services are ready.

## 8. Incident Documentation

Significant incidents should be documented in the company's internal issue tracking system.

Documentation may include:

- Incident description
- Affected users and systems
- Investigation findings
- Response actions
- Recovery activities
- Final resolution

## 9. Responsibilities

**IT Manager**
- Coordinates incident response activities.
- Determines incident severity.
- Communicates with executive management.

**IT Administrator**
- Investigates suspicious activity.
- Performs technical containment and recovery.
- Documents incident-related actions.

**Executive Management**
- Approves major business decisions.
- Coordinates external communications when necessary.

**Employees**
- Report suspicious activity.
- Cooperate with investigations.
- Follow security instructions.

## 10. Policy Maintenance

The IT Manager reviews this policy when significant changes occur to the company's systems, operations, or security environment.

Updates are communicated to relevant employees.
