# Cedar Ridge Services — Company Security Profile

**Assessment:** NIST CSF 2.0 Cybersecurity Gap Assessment  
**Industry:** IT and Business Services  
**Company Size:** 50 employees  
**Assessment Type:** Simulated Internal Security Assessment

## 1. Company Overview

Cedar Ridge Services is a fictional small business providing IT support, software development, and technology consulting services to other small businesses.

The company has approximately 50 employees working across engineering, IT, operations, sales, and administration. Employees primarily use company-managed laptops and cloud-based applications to support daily operations.

As the business grows, management wants to better understand its cybersecurity risks and determine whether existing security practices are sufficient to protect company and client information.

## 2. Technology Environment

The company relies on the following systems:

| System | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 | Email, collaboration, and employee accounts |
| GitHub | Source code management |
| Jira | Project and issue tracking |
| Confluence | Internal documentation |
| Cloud Infrastructure | Hosting business applications |
| Backup Systems | Data protection and recovery |

Most employees access these systems remotely or from the company office.

## 3. Sensitive Information and Business Risks

Cedar Ridge Services handles employee information, client records, internal documents, source code, and system credentials.

Key business concerns include:

- Unauthorized access to company or client information
- Compromised employee or administrator accounts
- Disruption of cloud services and business operations
- Loss of important company or client data
- Inadequate preparation for cybersecurity incidents

A security incident could result in operational downtime, financial losses, damaged client relationships, and reputational harm.

## 4. IT and Security Responsibilities

Cedar Ridge Services does not have a dedicated cybersecurity department. Security responsibilities are shared across its small IT team and management.

- **IT Manager:** Oversees IT operations and security priorities.
- **IT Administrator:** Manages employee accounts, devices, and system configurations.
- **Engineering Lead:** Oversees software development and source code security.
- **Operations Manager:** Coordinates vendors and operational continuity.
- **Executive Management:** Approves budgets, business priorities, and risk decisions.

## 5. Current Security Practices

Management has provided the following preliminary information:

- MFA is enabled for some employee accounts.
- Company-managed laptops use endpoint protection.
- User access is managed by the IT team.
- Backups are performed regularly.
- An incident response document exists.
- Security awareness training is provided periodically.
- Vulnerability remediation is handled by IT.
- Security policies have not been consistently reviewed.

These statements are **unverified scenario assumptions**, not confirmed assessment findings. Supporting policies and evidence will be reviewed during the gap assessment.

## 6. Assessment Purpose

Management has requested an assessment using NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 to understand the company's current security posture.

The assessment will:

1. Evaluate selected security outcomes across the six NIST CSF functions.
2. Review existing policies and simulated supporting evidence.
3. Compare current security practices against the company's desired security posture.
4. Identify control gaps and associated business risks.
5. Recommend practical security improvements.
6. Develop a prioritized 90-day remediation roadmap for management.

## 7. Assessment Scope

The assessment will focus on:

- Cybersecurity governance and security policies
- Asset and risk management
- Identity and access management
- Security monitoring and vulnerability management
- Incident response preparedness
- Data backup and recovery

The assessment will evaluate approximately 20 selected NIST CSF 2.0 outcomes rather than every framework outcome.

**Limitations:** All company information, records, policies, and evidence are fictional. This project demonstrates a framework-based cybersecurity gap assessment and does not represent a real organizational audit or compliance certification.
